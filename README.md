# Utawakui

> 為歌回與翻唱準備而設計的 OBS 桌面輔助工具。

[繁體中文](#繁體中文) | [English](#english)

## 繁體中文

### 從準備到畫面呈現，集中在一處

Utawakui 協助直播主、VTuber 與歌回企劃者整理自己的音訊、安排演出內容，
並將播放資訊呈現在 OBS 畫面中。曲庫、歌詞與播放清單集中在同一個桌面工具，
讓準備與演出操作更連貫。

### Utawakui 可以協助你

- **整理歌曲**：匯入並分類電腦中的音訊，補充曲名、歌手與封面。
- **安排演出**：建立演出歌單、調整待播順序，快速切換上一首或下一首。
- **播放與練習**：控制播放進度、音量與循環，並調整升降調、播放速度及
  導唱音量。
- **準備歌詞**：整理一般或同步歌詞，供練習、演出與畫面顯示使用。
- **製作 OBS 畫面**：透過 OBS 內建的瀏覽器來源，顯示目前曲目、演出歌單、
  歌詞與封面。

### 資料與連線

音訊、歌詞、播放清單與處理結果主要保存在你的電腦。只有在你啟用或使用需要
連線的歌曲來源、歌詞查找或更新功能時，Utawakui 才會連線至相應服務。提供給
OBS 的播放資訊只在本機電腦內傳送。

## English

### Preparation and On-Screen Information in One Place

Utawakui helps streamers, VTubers, and karaoke or cover-song creators organize
their audio, plan performances, and present playback information in OBS. The
library, lyrics, and playlists stay together in one desktop tool for a smoother
experience before and during a performance.

### What Utawakui Helps You Do

- **Organize songs**: Import and sort audio from your computer, with titles,
  artists, and artwork.
- **Plan performances**: Build performance playlists, arrange the upcoming
  order, and move between songs quickly.
- **Play and practice**: Control position, volume, and repeat, change the song's
  key or playback speed, and choose how much vocal to keep as a guide.
- **Prepare lyrics**: Organize plain or timed lyrics for practice, performance,
  and on-screen display.
- **Build OBS views**: Use OBS's built-in browser source to show the current
  song, performance playlist, lyrics, and artwork.

### Your Data and Network Use

Audio, lyrics, playlists, and processed results are primarily stored on your
computer. Utawakui connects to other services only when you enable or use a
feature that needs an online song source, lyrics search, or update service.
Playback information sent to OBS stays on the same computer.
